# Contributing

Thanks for helping improve this preparation pack.

Useful contributions include:

- typo or formatting corrections;
- clearer explanations;
- original SRE practice scenarios;
- improvements to scoring criteria or preparation workflow; and
- reports of broken repository links.

Please do **not** submit confidential, leaked, or verbatim interview questions that you are not permitted to share.

For substantial content changes, open an issue first describing the proposed improvement and why it helps an SRE candidate.
